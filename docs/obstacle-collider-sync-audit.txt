PART 2: AUDIT AFTER FIX (REAL MEASURED VALUES WITH ObstacleColliderSync + FixedUpdate Physics.SyncTransforms)
Fix Mechanism:
  1. ObstacleAnimationController evaluates in FixedUpdate() at Time.fixedTime AND calls Physics.SyncTransforms().
  2. ObstacleColliderSync on each moving collider copies pose from matching mesh and calls rb.MovePosition/MoveRotation.
  3. In Update(), ObstacleAnimationController evaluates at Time.time and syncs transforms for smooth 144Hz+ rendering.
  4. Kinematic Rigidbody on each moving collider has isKinematic=true and continuous velocity calculation in PhysX.
